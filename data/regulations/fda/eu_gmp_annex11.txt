EU GMP Annex 11 — Computerised Systems
Source: European Medicines Agency

VALIDATION: The application should be validated; IT infrastructure should be
qualified. Where a computerised system replaces a manual operation, there
should be no resultant decrease in product quality.

AUDIT TRAILS: Consideration should be given to creating audit trails for
all changes to data, especially for systems in GMP critical processes.
Audit trails should be available and convertible to a human readable form.

ACCESS CONTROL: Physical and/or logical controls should be in place to
restrict access to computerised systems. Appropriate methods of access
restriction could include keys, pass cards, passwords, biometric data and
restricted access to computer equipment and storage areas.

DATA INTEGRITY: Data should be secured by physical and electronic means
against damage. Stored data should be checked for accessibility, readability
and accuracy. Access to data should be ensured throughout the retention period.

ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES: An electronic signature is a set of data in electronic
form that substitutes for a handwritten signature and is used to authenticate
a computer record. The identity of persons using electronic signatures must
be verified and their use authorised by competent authority.

21 CFR PART 11 vs EU GMP ANNEX 11 COMPARISON:
Both require: validation, audit trails, access control, electronic signatures.
EU Annex 11: requires supplier assessments, periodic reviews, disaster recovery.
21 CFR Part 11: more prescriptive on technical requirements for e-signatures.
