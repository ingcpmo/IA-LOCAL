TITLE: ICH Q10 Pharmaceutical Quality System
SOURCE: International Council for Harmonisation

PURPOSE: Describes a pharmaceutical quality system model that can be
implemented throughout the different stages of a product's lifecycle.

KEY ELEMENTS:
1. Management Responsibility: Senior management commitment to quality system
2. Continual Improvement: Mechanisms to identify and implement improvements
3. Process Performance and Product Quality Monitoring
4. CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) system
5. Change Management: systematic approach to evaluating proposed changes

CHANGE CONTROL:
All changes to validated systems/processes must go through change control:
- Description of proposed change and reason
- Risk assessment: impact on product quality, regulatory status
- Required actions: testing, validation, regulatory notification
- Approval before implementation
- Effectiveness check after implementation

CAPA PROCESS:
Corrective Action: eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity
Preventive Action: eliminate the cause of a potential nonconformity
CAPA must be: documented, risk-based, effective, and verified

PERIODIC REVIEW OF VALIDATED SYSTEMS:
Documented review at defined intervals (typically annual for critical systems)
Review should include:
- Changes made since last review
- Deviations and their resolution
- Calibration and maintenance records
- SOPs: current and followed
- Recommended improvements
Outcome: confirm system remains in validated state or initiate revalidation
