✓ 21 CFR 211.194(a) — Laboratory records (chromatograms)
  Texto: "Laboratory records shall include complete data derived from all
  tests necessary to assure compliance with established specifications and
  standards, including examinations and assays... All individual laboratory
  raw data, graphs, charts, and spectra from laboratory instruments, properly
  identified to show the specific component, drug product container, closure,
  in-process material, or drug product, and lot examined are retained."

NOTA IMPORTANTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Sin los PDFs de USP <621> y USP <1058>, el agente hplc_data_review
responde basado en su system prompt (criterios codificados):
- Tailing factor ≤2.0
- %RSD RT ≤1.0%, área ≤2.0%
- Rs ≥2.0
- R² ≥0.999 calibration curve
Estos criterios son correctos per USP pero sin corpus RAG la calidad
de respuesta será menor. Se recomienda poblar el corpus antes de producción.
